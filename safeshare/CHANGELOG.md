## [0.2.0] — 2026-09-27

- **Summary:** First marked release. Solid ink, a larger page for extra boxes, and this history.
- **Notes:** The shared photo paints opaque black over the letters and saves a new JPEG or PNG. A blur is not used, because a blur can sometimes be undone. Enlarge, Larger, Smaller, Fit, and Move make a hand-drawn box easier. A label value across a wide gap, or on the line under a colon, is covered. A date of birth like `1/2/80` is covered. Traditional Chinese and English OCR stay on the phone. Version stamps: `VERSION.txt`, `package.json`, Home, and Settings. History opens this file.
